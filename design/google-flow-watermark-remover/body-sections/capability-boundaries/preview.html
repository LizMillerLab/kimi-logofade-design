What LogoFade can and can't do today

What it does today

  • Removes the visible Gemini star from still images, exactly reversing the blend Gemini applied.（占位草稿）
  • Runs 100% in your browser: images are never uploaded, and no account is needed.
  • Accepts original PNG, JPEG and WebP exports from Gemini, from 512 px up to 4K.

What it doesn't do yet

  • Flow, Veo and Omni video exports — video is not processed yet.（占位草稿）
  • SynthID, the invisible watermark Google embeds in the image itself.
  • Text labels, other companies' logos, and watermarks on images that were resized or re-saved.
