Works with

  • Gemini app image exports

    Images you download from the Gemini website or the Gemini mobile app are what this tool is built for, from 512 px up to 4K.（占位草稿）

    The one condition is that the file is the original download.

  • Nano Banana generated images

    Pictures you generate or edit with Nano Banana in the Gemini app carry the same star in the same corner.（占位草稿）

    They are handled in exactly the same way as other Gemini exports.

  • Flow, Veo and Omni video exports (not yet)

    Flow, Veo and Omni export video, and LogoFade does not process video today.（占位草稿）

    If video support matters to you, email us — it is how we decide what to build next.
