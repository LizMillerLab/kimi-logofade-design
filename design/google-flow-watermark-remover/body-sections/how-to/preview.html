How to remove the Gemini watermark from a still image

  1. Step 1 — Open the image tool

    Open the LogoFade home page in your browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create.（占位草稿）

    The tool runs entirely in this browser tab, on your own device.

  2. Step 2 — Drop your original Gemini image

    Download the image from Gemini, then drag that file onto the box, or tap Choose an image. PNG, JPEG and WebP are accepted.（占位草稿）

    Use the file exactly as Gemini gave it to you: screenshots and resized copies will be turned down.

  3. Step 3 — Download the clean image

    Drag the divider to compare before and after, or zoom in on the corner where the star was.（占位草稿）

    The file is saved in the format you brought, at the same size, with every other pixel untouched.
