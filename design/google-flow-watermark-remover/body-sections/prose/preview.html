Flow, Veo, Omni and Gemini: which watermark is which

Gemini stamps still images with a pale four-pointed star in the bottom-right corner. That star is drawn at partial transparency over the finished picture, and because its pattern is known, it can be reversed exactly.（正文占位草稿，文案以内容侧定稿为准）

Flow, Veo and Omni are Google's video tools. Their exports carry their own marks, and video frames cannot be un-blended the same way a still image can, which is why LogoFade does not process them yet.

Separate from any visible mark, Google also embeds SynthID, an invisible watermark, into the file itself. LogoFade never touches SynthID, on images or on video.

If what you have is a still image from Gemini, the image tool removes the visible star in your browser, for free.
