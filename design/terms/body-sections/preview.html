The service

LogoFade is a free tool that removes the visible Gemini watermark from an image. It runs in your browser: the image is processed on your device and is not uploaded. You do not need an account, and there is nothing to pay.

Only use it on images you have the right to modify

You may use the tool only on images that you created, or that you have permission to edit. You use it at your own risk: you alone are responsible for the images you process, for what you do with the results, and for any consequences of that use.

That includes following the law where you live and the terms of the service that produced the image. Do not use a cleaned image to mislead people about where it came from in situations where you are required to disclose that it was made with AI, and do not use the tool to infringe anyone else’s rights.

What the tool does and does not do

The tool removes the visible star that Gemini places in the corner of an image. It does not remove SynthID, the invisible watermark embedded in the picture itself. The file you download is re-encoded by your browser, so metadata from the original image, such as EXIF data and GPS location, is not kept in the downloaded file.

The tool does not work on every image. When it cannot remove the watermark cleanly it gives an explanation instead of a result. Check each result yourself before you rely on it.
